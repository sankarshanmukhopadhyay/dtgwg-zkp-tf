# v0.4 foundational-frame ratification record

## Source

- **Authority:** DTG ZKP Task Force maintainer discussion
- **Source:** [Discussion #13 — Toward v0.4: candidate sections to progress in the requirements doc](https://github.com/trustoverip/dtgwg-zkp-tf/discussions/13)
- **Recorded date:** 2026-08-12

## Outcome recorded by the source discussion

Discussion #13 states that the v0.3 requirements predate ratification of the foundational frame and identifies the following as settled for progression into v0.4:

- privacy-not-assurance;
- context-dependent unlinkability;
- the Minimum Liveness / Extended Personhood profile split;
- paired assurance and disclosure boundaries; and
- the rule that material claims name their adversary and horizon.

The same discussion describes B1 as an **adopted working definition** and B2 as a **working position**. They are therefore recorded separately as `adopted-working-position`, not as ratified decisions.

## Impact

The root requirements document now presents A1–A5 as adopted foundations rather than open framing. B1 and B2 are presented as working positions with explicit context, role-separation, adversary, horizon and evidence requirements. Construction selection remains downstream of these semantic and boundary decisions.
