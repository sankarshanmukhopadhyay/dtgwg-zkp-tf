## Interpretation

`implemented-as-working-baseline` means the fork contains documentation, schemas, fixtures or tests based on the proposed position. It does not mean the position has been ratified upstream. A transition to `ratified` or `ratified-with-amendment` requires a ratification record identifying the authority, source, date and resulting impact review.
